Privacy Policy

Ezer.app — Last updated November 2026

SOUZA APPS

Ezer.app was created to help people grow spiritually. This policy explains, in plain terms, what data we collect, why we collect it, and how we protect it.

1. Who we are

Ezer.app is developed by Gabriel Lima de Souza (Souza Apps), an independent developer based in Brazil. Questions: suporte.ezer.app@gmail.com

2. Data we collect

Account data — when you create an account, you provide:

  • Your name and email address (sign in with email, Google, or Facebook)
  • A profile photo (optional, supplied by your social sign-in provider)

Usage and engagement data — collected automatically to improve the app:

  • When you open or return to the app
  • Which devotional series you started or finished
  • When you create a partnership, a group, or a fast

This data is linked to your user ID, but never to the content of your personal reflections or chat messages.

Content you create — stored so you can access it from other devices:

  • Personal journal reflections (encrypted in transit)
  • Chat messages in groups and partnerships
  • Prayer requests shared in a partnership
  • Reports you submit: the reason, the reported content (we keep a copy of the text for review), the date, and your user identifier
  • The list of people you have blocked

Technical data — collected by third-party services:

  • Push notifications: the device token (Firebase / FCM on Android; the Apple Push Notification service, sent through Expo, on iPhone)
  • Ads (Google AdMob): the device advertising identifier (on iPhone, only if you allow tracking in the system prompt)
  • Browser version (ezerapp.blog/app): the site stores your preferences, progress, and login session in your browser's local storage. When Google AdSense ads are active, Google may use cookies and identifiers to show and measure ads

3. How we use your data

  • To provide and improve the app's features
  • To send reminders and partnership or group activity notifications
  • To analyze which series are used most (in aggregate)
  • To show relevant ads to free-plan users
  • To review reports and protect the community from offensive or abusive content

We do not sell your data to third parties. We do not use your data for external marketing.

4. Who we share data with

  • Supabase — database and authentication (servers in São Paulo, Brazil)
  • Google Firebase — push notifications on Android
  • Expo and Apple — push notification delivery on iPhone
  • Google AdMob — advertising (free plan only)
  • Google AdSense — advertising in the browser version
  • GitHub Pages — hosting for the website and the browser version
  • RevenueCat — purchase and Pro plan management

All of these partners follow their own privacy policies, which are compatible with Brazil's LGPD (General Data Protection Law).

5. Data retention

  • Usage data (app_aberto): kept for 90 days, then deleted automatically
  • Started and finished series data: kept indefinitely for the app's statistics
  • Chat and prayer requests from ended partnerships: deleted 24 hours after the partnership ends
  • Reports: deleted automatically after 12 months. If the account of the person who filed a report is deleted, that person's report is deleted along with it. Reports about someone's content may remain, within those 12 months, after that person's account is deleted
  • Block list: kept as long as the account exists
  • Account and personal data: kept as long as the account exists

6. Your rights (LGPD)

At any time, you can:

  • Access the data we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct inaccurate data
  • Ask us to delete your account and all of your data
  • Withdraw your consent to the use of your data

You can delete your account in the app (Settings, My Account, Delete my account) or follow the instructions at ezerapp.blog/delete-account.html. For any other rights, contact us at suporte.ezer.app@gmail.com.

7. Security

All data is transmitted with TLS encryption. The database uses Row Level Security (RLS), which ensures that each user can access only their own data.

8. Children

Ezer.app is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a minor has provided data without a parent's or guardian's consent, please contact us.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Significant changes will be announced in the app. The date at the top of this page shows the version in effect.
