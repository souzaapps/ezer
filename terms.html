4. Reporting, blocking, and moderation

To keep these spaces safe, the app offers the following tools:

  • Report: press and hold a message, or tap the flag next to a partnership prayer request, and choose "Report." The content stops appearing for you right away.
  • Block: you can block a person and stop seeing their messages and prayer requests. To unblock, go to Settings, Blocked users.
  • Automatic hiding: in groups, a message reported by more than one person is hidden from everyone until it is reviewed. In a partnership, a report hides the content for both people.
  • Group creator: the person who created a group can delete messages in that group.
  • Word filter: the app blocks messages that contain offensive terms. The filter is not perfect, which is why reports matter.

The Ezer.app team reviews reports as soon as possible. To report a problem or request the removal of content, write to suporte.ezer.app@gmail.com.
