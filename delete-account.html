Delete your account and data

Ezer.app — Last updated November 2026

SOUZA APPS

You can delete your Ezer.app account, and the data linked to it, at any time. This page explains how, in the app or by email.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Ezer.app and sign in to your account.
  2. Tap the Settings icon (the gear at the top of the home screen).
  3. Tap My Account.
  4. At the bottom of the screen, tap Delete my account.
  5. Read the warning and confirm with Delete permanently.

The deletion happens immediately and cannot be undone. The option appears only for people who are signed in.

What is deleted

  • Your profile, name, and sign-up email
  • Your progress: devotionals read, series, the Flame, and your reading plan
  • Your reflections, favorites, and preferences saved to your account
  • Your membership in groups, partnerships, and group reading plans
  • Groups where you are the only administrator (they are deleted for all members)

What may remain

  • Reports that other people filed about your messages may be kept for up to 12 months, only for community safety, and are then deleted automatically.
  • Data stored only on your device or in your browser (such as web version preferences) is not on our servers. To remove it, uninstall the app or clear the site data for ezerapp.blog in your browser.
  • Purchases made through Google Play or the App Store are not canceled by deleting your account. If you have a subscription, cancel it in the store itself.

By email, if you cannot sign in to the app

If you cannot open the app or prefer to request deletion in writing, send an email to suporte.ezer.app@gmail.com with the subject Account Deletion, using the same email as your account. For your security, we may ask you to confirm that the account is yours. We complete the removal within 48 business hours and reply to let you know.

More information

See how we handle your data in the Privacy Policy and the rules of use in the Terms of Use.
